# Product Overview

Blast Wallet is an AI-powered self-custody Web3 wallet designed to securely manage digital assets, connect to decentralized applications, and provide intelligent blockchain security.

The wallet gives users full ownership of their private keys while providing a modern and secure Web3 experience.

---

# Vision

Build one of the world's most secure and intelligent Web3 wallets.

---

# Mission

Enable anyone to safely access blockchain technology without sacrificing ownership, privacy, or security.
